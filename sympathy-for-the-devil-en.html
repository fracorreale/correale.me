The Rolling Stones · Beggars Banquet · 1968

The Master and Margarita by Mikhail Bulgakov was one of Mick Jagger’s inspirations for Sympathy for the Devil, released by the Rolling Stones in 1968 on Beggars Banquet. Marianne Faithfull recalled introducing Jagger to the novel and discussing it with him at length.

The narrator is Lucifer: polished, charming and present at some of history’s most violent events. The song can be read as a provocation about the nature of evil: this seductive devil invites us to recognise human responsibility in the atrocities he recounts. That blend of charm, irony and unease brings it close to Bulgakov’s world.
