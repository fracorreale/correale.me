THE STORY

Before everything changed

I was happy in Naples. I had no intention of leaving. And then, something changed.

In 1999, I was sixteen and perfectly happy with my life in Naples. I had my friends, my routines and no particular reason to imagine spending the last two years of high school somewhere else.

I applied for a scholarship to UWC Adriatic, passed the regional selection and was invited to the national round. At first, I wasn't particularly keen on the idea. Why would I want to leave a life I liked?

Then came the national selections: three days with other sixteen-year-olds from all over Italy. There was nothing international about the atmosphere yet. We were all Italian, and it felt almost like a short study holiday.

But something clicked during those three days. I can't point to one particular moment. I only know that I arrived unconvinced and left feeling differently about the possibility of going to Duino.

I was awarded the scholarship. And the next chapter of my life would take place in a small village near Trieste.
