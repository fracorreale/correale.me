THE STORY

A world in one room

When I arrived in Duino, my new home was Lucchese, Room No. 3. There were five of us sharing it, each from a different country.

I was the Italian. Santiago came from Ecuador, Yew Hock from Malaysia, Mono from Lesotho and Charles from Zambia. Five countries across four continents, all under the same roof.

Five countries. Four continents. One room.

And Room No. 3 was only the beginning. On the same floor were Matteo, João and Tom, and in another room Alberto, Andrea, Artan and Stanislav. A remarkable mix of people had suddenly become my everyday life.

It was a rather unusual way to begin the final two years of school. And that was precisely what made it special.
