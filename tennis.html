01 / ON COURT

A long-running affair.

I started at ten, took a rather long changeover between sixteen and thirty, and eventually found my way back. Today I play once or twice a week, mostly singles with friends at Harbour Club Milano. Competitive? Yes. Entirely at peace with my game? Working on it.

02 / THE GAME PLAN

Attack first.
Figure it out later.

I play aggressively, even on the points that matter. I like opponents who attack too, and tend to play better against stronger players. A higher bar helps me find my game.

04 / WHAT STAYS

Sunday mornings.
And the next generation.

One of my favourite memories is playing with my father on Sunday mornings. Now my daughter is starting to play. The game changes, the challenge continues — and there is something lovely about seeing it begin again.
