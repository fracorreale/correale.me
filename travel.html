Most of it is the same hour, over and over.

Seen one at a time, a boarding pass is just a morning that started early. Put years of them on a map and a shape appears: a few cities I keep returning to, a handful of journeys that were genuinely far, and one airport that every line eventually comes back to. The long trips are the ones I remember. The short ones are the ones that made the years.
